The four domains

Every city system, read on its own terms

Each module is trained on the data that domain actually produces, then normalized into the same executive format: forecast, priority, action.

Transportation & Safety

Forecasts congestion and incident risk by district, so traffic and safety teams know where to route attention before it becomes a problem.

Model output → risk level + hotspot list

Energy & Buildings

Predicts load and consumption patterns across the grid and municipal buildings to support capacity planning and efficiency programs.

Model output → demand curve + alerts

Governance & Digital Services

Tracks service-request volume and response quality across departments, surfacing where digital services need attention first.

Model output → service score + priority

Environment & Waste

Forecasts monthly collection volume by borough and district, turning tonnage trends into a clear planning outlook and next action.

Model output → volume forecast + plan

From raw signal to decision

How a single forecast gets made

01

Collect & profile

The city profile (population, districts, strategic goals) and domain history set the context every model reads against.

02

Run the domain model

A model trained specifically for that domain — transportation, energy, governance or waste — produces a forecast and a confidence signal.

03

Translate to a decision

The forecast is converted into a headline, a priority level and 2–3 concrete actions a planner can hand to their team.

Under the hood

Built as an interactive dashboard, not a slideshow

The full experience — profile setup, live forecasts, an AI advisor and downloadable reports — runs as a Streamlit application backed by per-domain ML models.

Interactive dashboard

Streamlit + Plotly power the live forecast views, KPI cards and the AI advisor chat.

Independent ML models

Each domain ships its own trained model bundle, so a change in one domain never touches another.

Executive reporting

Every completed assessment can be exported as a stakeholder- ready report in one click.
